You can hide widgets you don't use from the Operations page. This reduces clutter and helps you focus on the information that matters to your workflow.

## What You'll Learn

- How to hide widgets from the Operations page
- Where to find the widget visibility settings

---

## Hide Widgets

<Steps>
<Step title="Open General Settings">
Navigate to **top-right user menu > MainWP Settings > General Settings** (`/wp-admin/admin.php?page=Settings`).
</Step>
<Step title="Find widget visibility option">
Locate **Show widgets** in the **General Settings** section.
</Step>
<Step title="Select widgets to hide">
Clear the checkbox next to each widget you want to hide from the Operations page.
</Step>
<Step title="Save your changes">
Click **Save Settings**. Widgets with cleared checkboxes no longer appear on the Operations page.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Restore Hidden Widgets

To show a hidden widget again:

1. Return to **top-right user menu > MainWP Settings > General Settings** (`/wp-admin/admin.php?page=Settings`)
2. Select the widget's checkbox under **Show widgets**
3. Click **Save Settings**
