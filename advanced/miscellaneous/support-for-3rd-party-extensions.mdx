Some MainWP add-ons were developed by other companies and are labeled as **3rd Party add-ons**. Support for third-party add-ons is provided by their developers, not by MainWP.

## What You'll Learn

- Which add-ons are developed by third parties
- Where to get support for each third-party add-on

---

## Why MainWP Cannot Support Third-Party Add-ons

MainWP does not provide support for third-party add-ons because we don't have:

- The specialized training for each product
- Access to the source code
- Knowledge of the developer's implementation decisions

For the best support experience, contact the add-on developer directly.
