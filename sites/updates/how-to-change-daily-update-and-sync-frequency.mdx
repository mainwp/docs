MainWP Dashboard has separate schedules for automatic synchronization and advanced automatic updates. Sync refreshes child-site data, including available updates. The automatic-update schedule controls when MainWP installs eligible updates.

## What You'll Learn

- Configure the daily sync time and frequency
- Schedule automatic updates daily, weekly, or monthly
- Understand how the update delay and cron affect timing

## Prerequisites

- MainWP Dashboard installed and activated
- Access to Dashboard settings

---

## Configure Sync Frequency

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to Settings">
Go to **top-right user menu > MainWP Settings > General Settings** (`/wp-admin/admin.php?page=Settings`).
</Step>
<Step title="Find General Settings">
On the **General Settings** tab, locate the **General Settings** section.
</Step>
<Step title="Set daily sync time">
Find **Daily sync time** and set your preferred start time.

This determines when MainWP begins the first automatic sync of the day.
</Step>
<Step title="Set sync frequency">
Set **Frequency of auto sync** to the number of sync runs per day. Options range from **Once per day** to **Twelve times per day**; the default is **Twice per day**.

| Option | Sync Times |
|--------|------------|
| Once per day | At the configured sync time |
| Twice per day | Sync time + 12 hours later |
| Three times per day | Every 8 hours starting at sync time |
</Step>
<Step title="Save">
Click **Save Settings**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Configure Update Frequency

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Updates Settings">
Open **top-right user menu > MainWP Settings > General Settings** (`/wp-admin/admin.php?page=Settings`) and find the **Updates Settings** section.
</Step>
<Step title="Enable the update types you need">
Set the relevant control to **Install Trusted Updates**:

- **Plugin advanced automatic updates**
- **Theme advanced automatic updates**
- **Translation advanced automatic updates**
- **WP Core advanced automatic updates**

Mark plugins or themes as trusted on their **Advanced Auto Updates** pages. WordPress core also requires **Auto Update Core** enabled for each site. Follow the [Manage Updates guide](/sites/updates/manage-updates) for these steps and translation eligibility.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the schedule">
Set **Frequency of automatic updates** to **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**. For weekly or monthly runs, use **Select day** to choose the weekday or day of the month. Set **Select time** for the update run.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the update delay">
Choose **Advanced automatic updates delay**. The default is **1 day**; available options are **Delay off**, **1 day** through **7 days**, **14 days**, and **30 days**. An update must meet the delay before it is eligible for a scheduled run.
</Step>
<Step title="Save the schedule">
Click **Save Settings**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How Timing Works

For example, configure:

- **Daily sync time**: 7:00 AM
- **Frequency of auto sync**: Three times per day
- **Frequency of automatic updates**: Daily
- **Select time**: 11:30 PM

MainWP schedules syncs for 7:00 AM, 3:00 PM, and 11:00 PM. The automatic-update run is scheduled separately for 11:30 PM, subject to the enabled update types, trust settings, ignored updates, and update delay.

Use the **Timezone** configured in MainWP Dashboard when choosing these times. Scheduled times depend on cron execution; a late cron trigger can delay a run.

---

## Important Notes

- Increasing sync frequency does not increase automatic-update frequency.
- Plugin and theme advanced automatic updates require both the global setting and trusted items. Ignored items are excluded.
- The update delay and the next scheduled run can both affect when an eligible update is installed; there is no universal 24-hour completion deadline.
- MainWP uses cron for background work. Follow [Manage WP Cron on a Low-Traffic MainWP Dashboard](/customization/manage-wp-cron-on-low-traffic-mainwp-dashboard) for reliable cron execution.
